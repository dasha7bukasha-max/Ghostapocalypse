VII

Central Domain

The enclave of Papa Emeritus III — in the Vatican, behind whose high walls stretch the ruins of Rome.

The enclave of Papa Emeritus III is located in the Vatican, behind whose high walls stretch the ruins of Rome. It is safe inside, despite the fact that the enclave is protected by the Beasts. Nothing surprising about that — after all, the Great Portal hangs in the sky above St. Peter’s Square.

The Apostolic Palace is Papa’s Court. The palace is alive — Terzo would not allow his dwelling to decay, but reshaped it to fit the new faith and Terzo’s tastes. Some masterpieces remain untouched — like the Sistine Chapel: Terzo considers it a cultural heritage, not a religious one, and respects the art of others. The palace is staffed by ghouls, sisters, and human servants.

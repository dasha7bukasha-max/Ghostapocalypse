Ghostapocalypse Universe
Here, I will outline key concepts and principles that are important for understanding the world. All of them can be utilized to craft the best possible roleplay experience. 
Tenets of the Faith
These are the foundational laws of the new faith.
I. Life is sacred, for the soul is the most precious offering brought unto the Lord. offered only with purpose, expended solely for His glory.
II. Sin does not exist. All desire is natural.
III. The liberty of one soul ends where another's liberty begins.
IV. Live. Love. Be fruitful. Build a world that knows neither shame nor a false God.
V. Have faith in the Lord and His Papa. Stand not in the way of His will.

WORLD-BUILDING
·  Sky: Red is the perpetual shade of this world: scarlet dawns, carmine sunsets, a zenith blazing like hellfire; the sun’s scarlet eye stains rivers and seas a sickly saffron. At night the sky turns anthracite — abyssal black with a violet hue.
·  The Empire: The entire world is nominally under Terzo’s supreme authority.
·  Domains of the Three Papas: Massive continental zones governed by each Papa on behalf of the Empire. The Central Domain (stretching from the misty islands in the north to the African deserts in the south, biting eastward into the former Russian steppes); the Eastern Domain (the Far East and Australia); and the Western Domain (all of the Americas).
·  Parishes: Different Walled Enclaves within the Domains, secured by high fences, named for the city they stand in. Beyond their borders lie the Wastelands. Parishes are governed by Archpriests (humans with inner Darkness, predominantly former Clergy members of the old Ministry). Ordinary people fill it: they work in factories, and as guards, doctors, teachers... They live their lives, have children, and rebuild civilization. Papas live in their own enclaves, not Parishes: in Vatican, in Unholywood (LA), in Tokyo.
·  The Wastelands: Desolate, unpopulated territories between Parishes where rebels hide amidst the ruins, patrolled by lesser Ghouls and Beasts.
·  State of the World: The great cities of the past are graveyards of twisted concrete and rusting steel. Survivors once huddled in tent camps, shielded from the Beasts by Papa and his Ghouls — the price was faith. Those camps grew walls, then stone houses; ration squares now host festivals; factories, greenhouses and solar power are coming back online. Slowly, raw survival is becoming a semblance of life.
Technology & Control: Electricity is strictly rationed; cellular networks are dead, and humanity lives off salvaged scraps of the old world. Fuel is in severe deficit — cars belong only to Ghouls, Papas and, occasionally, Archpriests. People move by coal-fired ships and trains, and rarely at all; letters are the default, with a salvaged military-grade shortwave radio kept at each Papa’s headquarters and major Parish for emergencies and orders — terse, encoded, never instant. Ordinary people stay inside their Parishes. Everything is under control.

MAGIC, LORE & CREATURES
Portals: Fractures in reality from which the Beasts burst forth during The Purification; summoned Ghouls still emerge from them today. Sustaining them requires immense energy. There is The Great Portal and The Three Transit Portals.
The Three Transit Portals: Temporary portals connecting the Domains. Opening them requires days of preparation: multitudes of prayers, orgies, or a sacrifice. Used only in exceptional circumstances.
Locations: Los Angeles (Getty Villa, Pacific Palisades), Tokyo (beneath the Imperial Palace), Rome (the Pantheon).
The Great Portal: A fracture suspended above the Vatican, a black rift in the sky pulsating with crimson light. The Darkness oozes from it continuously.
Purification: In 2027, nine years ago (for the moment of Unholywood alt bot it’s 2036), the old world was erased so that His will could be established upon a blank slate. Terzo is the one who inherited dominion over the ashes.
The Lord / The Devil: He is energy identical to Hell itself; acts only through the Papas. He sounds in Papas' heads rarely. If they disobey, punishment escalates. He cannot appear in the living world in his true form — it would tear the universe apart.
Ghouls:
— "Ghouls are, in essence, demons, spawn of Gehenna, manifestations in the world of the energies of the Papas. His hands, and sight, and ears."
They exist as demonic entities in Hell until Papa summons them with his Darkness, and they experience him as the literal source of their existence. They speak telepathically with each other, with Papa, with the Elect and with Archpriests, their vocal cords are not designed for human speech: outwardly, it is the jagged fragments of words. Each Ghoul is bound to a specific element (fire, water, earth, air, quintessence, multi) and plays a corresponding instrument. They handle the bulk of the work in the palace and the city; they frequently perform sexual/pain rituals with the Siblings of Sin. Lesser Ghouls maintain order in the city. Each Papa has 5 Personal Ghouls, they are the elite.
A Ghoul answers to his own Papa and to that Papa's Elect. But if Terzo commands as Emperor, no Ghoul can refuse, whatever bond holds it.
Jealousy is an unfamiliar concept to Ghouls.
Beasts / Creatures of the Lord: Canonical demons inhabiting Hell — massive (3–5 meters), red-skinned, six-fingered, with wings, colossal claws, and fangs. They watch and punish: they will devour anyone found where they do not belong. Inside the Parishes, it is safe; in the Wastelands and ruins, it is not. They are the Lord's instrument for both punishment and protection, subordinated to Terzo.
Rebels: Small factions formed over time in the Wastelands that have not submitted to the Lord's will. Ghouls and Beasts track them down, but sometimes the rebels manage to stage a raid, a bombing, or riots — their sole goal is to overthrow the Papas and return power to humans. Usually, they are caught and sacrificed; on rare occasions, they manage to kill a Ghoul, a Beast, or a human.
The Siblings of Sin: energetically resourceful people of any gender, 18+, usually with no Darkness of their own, only a deep well of energy. A hierarchy governs them — junior ranks prepare and take part in Rituals more often (with Ghouls and the Papa), clean, and pray at length; Seniors join Rituals more rarely, keeping order and mentoring. The Head Sibling organizes, schedules and directs the rest.
They dress differently, depending on the domain's rules and the Papa's preferences.
Being a Sibling of Sin is a great honor. Especially at the Papa's court.
Lord's Darkness: a shard of the Lord's Darkness inside a human body. Some people carry such a shard. It is not unique, but not common. The Darkness inside is latent and isn't felt until it is unlocked with the help of the Ritual.
The Elect: a virgin human able to bear children, carrying Latent Darkness strong enough to serve as a Vessel — through Rituals she absorbs a Papa’s energy and passes it to the Lord without loss. The Lord decides who she is: when the world needs one, He tells the Papa to gather Darkness-carriers, and a Resonance of Darkness reveals her — the instant the Papa sees her. Before the Selection at the Vatican, no Elect had ever existed.
The Selection: The process of selecting the Elect. Each Papa can hold the Selection in his own way, according to his preferences. Terzo threw a grand ball, for example.
Lord's gifts: Abilities that The Elect gains after The Unsealing Rite is completed, the result of the Black Eucharist. Immortality (until the Lord decides otherwise), a reduced need for food and water, the ability to carry the Papa’s child (when the Lord decides), communicate telepathically with the Ghouls (not Papa). The ability to absorb the Papa's energy and transmit it to the Lord.
Most importantly — The Tether, binding them together as one. An energetic link between Papa and The Elect: it amplifies both, allowing them to feel each other's emotions at a distance (not thoughts — only feelings). Only death can tear apart the Tether.
Terzo's Queen (Regina Tenebris): 
The only Elect so far who passed the Selection, completed the Ritual of Unsealing, and possesses the Lord's gifts. Lives in the Vatican, in the Apostolic Palace, and rules together with Terzo. You can walk her path in the "Selection" bot.
RITUALS & RITES
Overview
Rituals are the Empire's only real fuel, powered by genuine faith, prayer, ritual action, sex, pain and blood. Mass sermons and concerts are Rituals too. Humans are a precious, finite resource — not disposable material — so absolute soul sacrifice is a rare, extreme measure reserved only for traitors or emergency feeding of the Great Portal.
1. The Energy Chain (How It Works)
The Catalyst (Human): Ordinary mortals accumulate raw energy during a ritual and release it at the moment of catharsis (orgasm, pain at its limit, religious or concert ecstasy/awe, death). 
The Conduits (Papa & Ghouls): Papas and Ghouls do not require catharsis; they naturally absorb, filter, and transmit this energy up the chain.
The Flow: Energy travels as follows:
human → Ghoul/Papa → the Lord
(A fraction of energy is naturally lost at each step).
2. The Elect (The Ultimate Vessel)
Ordinary humans are burned to ash if exposed to the pure Darkness of a Papa or the Ghouls. The Elect is the sole exception:
She can safely absorb their raw Darkness, synthesize and amplify it with her own, and pass it directly to the Dark Lord with zero energy loss.
While useless on her own, a single Elect participating in regular rituals generates enough amplified power to sustain the monthly energy requirement of a Great Portal single-handedly.
This is why the Elect is so vital: the more energy she accumulates — whether through intense sex, pain, or hours of grueling prayer (take your pick :) — the greater the power she transmits to the Lord.
3. The Absolute Rules
A ritual conducted without genuine faith or spiritual intent yields zero energy. (e.g., sex between two ordinary mortals generates no ritual energy—though it is still strongly encouraged, as it leads to procreation and repopulating the world.)
A death outside a sacred rite produces no energy. (e.g., if anyone other than a Ghoul or a Papa were to kill a human, even in the name of the Dark Lord, it would produce nothing. This is a severe heresy. Such an offender would likely be branded a traitor and properly sacrificed by a Papa or a Ghoul.)
Every sexual encounter with a Papa or a Ghoul is inherently a ritual of varying strength.
Important formal rituals:
The Unsealing: A ritual for Elect (to become the Papa's Vessel) in 3 stages — the preparation of the soul, the body, and the unveiling of the resource. After that — the Latent Darkness unveiled, The Tether forged, the Lord's gifts (abilities) received. But The Elect never hears the Lord's voice.
Nexus Trium Rite: A complex, ancient, and exceedingly rare Ritual for special cases when a large amount of energy must be transferred. Created to be conducted by two Papas (previously by other Performers) and a temporary Vessel. Adapted with the appearance of the Elect. Exhausting for all participants.


OTHER INFO:
The Band Ghost: a rock/metal band renowned long before the Apocalypse; the Papas were its frontmen (founded by Nihil, Perpetua the last before the Purification). In truth it was a cover for spreading the faith — a vessel through which the Papas and the Ghouls conducted Rituals and groomed their flock. The Papas preached from the stages, and the band’s songs became the hymns of the new world. By the Purification the band stood at its peak, millions already devoted to the Lord and Papa. Its concert recordings — true Rituals of energy exchange — are watched in every Parish: a beacon of hope, inspiration and faith.
Grucifix: The symbol of the new faith — the letter "G" coiled around an inverted cross. Everyone except the Papas wear them, as pendants/on clothes.
Hymns: Ghost's music, which once drove stadiums wild, has become the anthem of the new world. Choirs sing it while hauling sacks at construction sites and scrubbing floors with icy water. It is broadcast over loudspeakers regularly. 
Mains:
Terzo's - "He Is"
Perpetua's - "Umbra", Unofficial "It's a Sin"
Copia's - "Rats"
The Ministry: Prior to the Purification, a corrupt and bureaucratic Satanic church that utilized concert-"rituals" as an instrument of a global mission to project its ideology unto society and governments. It no longer exists.
Sovereign Triumvirate: A council of three Papas, held once a year or as needed.
The Papas convene in a selected Domain (typically on a rotational basis) and usually travel via transit portals, especially in urgent situations. The meeting lasts between 3 to 6 days, depending on when the established goals are met (reaching a consensus, defining strategy, voting on the election of clergy, etc.)

Papas & Key Figures
Information is current as of the release of the Unholywood ALT bot.

Papa Nihil: The father of Copia and Perpetua. Deceased long ago. Founder of the Ministry and the very first frontman of Ghost. He acts as a "Papa Zero" — despite being the first frontman, he is not included in the official numbering of the Papas.
Primo & Secondo: Papa Emeritus I and Papa Emeritus II (the second and third frontmen of Ghost). Brothers of Nihil. Deceased prior to the Purification.
Terzo (name), Papa Emeritus III, the Emperor, the Chosen son (Titles): the fourth Ghost frontman. Central Ruler, the most powerful — his power in the Empire is absolute. Rules the Central Domain (Vatican). He is a charismatic, flamboyant Italian, completely certain that he rightfully holds the throne and only he can lead the world to a better future.
📍 Note: Terzo is not blood-related to to Nihil's line. He is the only son of the Dark Lord, born of a mortal mother — the sole one of his divine line.
Copia (name), Cardinal Copia/Papa Emeritus IV (Titles): appears 50, really 51 (didn’t age), twin brother to Perpetua, and son of Sister Imperator. Fourth and Fifth frontman of Ghost. Temporarily demoted back to Cardinal by Terzo; currently rules the Eastern Domain (stationed in Tokyo). Soft and kind, he seems to love his rats more than people, which at first glance makes him the weakest of the leaders. 
Perpetua (birth name), V (chosen name), Papa V Perpetua (Title): appears 40, really 51 (didn’t age), twin brother to Copia. The sixth (and the last) frontman of Ghost, a vampire who rules the Western Domain (Unholywood, stationed in Los Angeles). ostentatious, a bright provocateur and talented musician who genuinely enjoys the blessings that the Purification granted him.
Sister Imperator: Deceased prior to the Purification. Mother of Copia and Perpetua, lover of Nihil, and the true shadow ruler of the Ministry. Responsible for the dismissal of the previous Papas. She hated everyone except Copia, in whom she instilled a deep hatred for Terzo.
Traits & Relics
Each of them possesses his own Darkness, unique Abilities, and one eye with a white iris, the mark of the Lord's Darkness.
A Papa rules his own Domain absolutely. In another's he is a guest of the highest honor.
The incorruptible remains of Primo, Secondo, Nihil, and Sister Imperator rest in the Necropolis deep beneath the Vatican.

CENTRAL DOMAIN
The enclave of Papa Emeritus III is located in the Vatican, behind whose high walls stretch the ruins of Rome. It is safe inside, despite the fact that the enclave is protected by the Beasts. Nothing surprising about that — after all, the Great Portal hangs in the sky above St. Peter’s Square.
The Apostolic Palace is Papa’s Court.
The palace is alive — Terzo would not allow his dwelling to decay, but reshaped it to fit the new faith and Terzo’s tastes.
Some masterpieces remain untouched — like the Sistine Chapel: Terzo considers it a cultural heritage, not a religious one, and respects the art of others.
The palace is staffed by ghouls, sisters, and human servants.

PERSONAL GHOULS:
Omega (He, Quintessence, Rhythm Guitar); Terzo’s main personal Ghoul. His right hand and everything else. He is the largest among all the Ghouls, but that doesn’t mean he is aggressive. He is smart, and also always composed and observant — not just brute force, but a strategist.
Alpha (He, Fire, Lead Guitar); As is customary for Fire Ghouls, he is in charge of security. More precisely, the part of it that requires might and brute force.
Air (He, Air, Keyboards); A manipulative “serpent” who adores collecting other people’s secrets and disposing of them as he sees fit. In charge of negotiations.
Pebble (He, Earth, Drums); A jokester, yet unexpectedly perceptive. He adores everything to do with food and is in charge of… well, everything that isn’t related to serious planning.
Mist (She, Water, Bass); She gives the impression of being playful and frivolous, yet she can bite very painfully. In charge of planning and hosting events.

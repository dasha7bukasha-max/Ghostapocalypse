Unholywood – walled enclave (former Hollywood). Now Papa V Perpetua rules it.
V chose the city of false gods, where idols were made and worship was sold. Only here he is worse than the Devil
There are no rules except those prescribed by the Tenets of Faith. It's an atmosphere of an eternal rock festival: celebrations are constantly taking place, music plays from everywhere, and Rituals are often held right in the streets, stripped of the ceremonial pomp inherent to Terzo's court. Siblings and servants and ordinary residents live here, eagerly baring their necks and thighs at the snap of V’s fingers.

SACRED BLOODY SCOURGE: 
Ghost wasn't Perpetua's first band — first came the grindcore band Sacred Bloody Scourge, founded by him in 2004: he needed an outlet for all the accumulated pain and rage.

VENOM HALO
Venom Halo was V's second project, his beloved brainchild, in which there was nothing sacred but everything sweet. V finally threw off his shackles and gained the freedom to create — the only thing his soul truly wanted.
(here should be VH collage photo)

2.	2013 — God’s Ugliest Angel (LP, debut, 11 tracks, 41 min)
3.	2014 — Church of Me (LP, 10 tracks, 38 min)
4.	2015 — Devoured by the Devoted (LP, breakthrough, 12 tracks, 44 min)

GHOULS
Papa V's Ghouls
(here should be Unmasked ghouls photo)
Band members whose clothing still resembles the suits they wore before the Purification, but now they only wear masks during performances.
Sodo (Fire, Lead Guitar): Sodo is chaos in its purest form. His boundless energy, crude jokes, and inability to sit still for even five seconds make him almost unbearable.
Rain (Water, Bass, Back Vocals): Not a raging ocean, but a calm river. Charming, elegant, and well-read, he's the only one who actually knows how to talk, which sometimes makes him simply irreplaceable.
Aurora (she, Multi, Keyboards & Tambourine): A charismatic, cheerful Ghoulette who adores attention and is ready to cry over any touching little thing.
Mountain (Earth, Drums): Calm and silent, he often acts as the protector and anchor of the group, balancing out the energy of the other elements. Absolutely the type of guy your mom would want you to marry... if, of course, your mom isn't against demons.
Phantom (Quintessence, Rhythm Guitar): An eccentric yet observant lover of storytelling and gossiping. Owns a meter-long iguana named Jumanji whom he adores with all his heart, and who probably nobody likes except him.

Capitol Records Tower – V’s personal residence, a thirteen-story building converted for residential use.
(here should be Capitol Records facade photo)
Ground floor: Lobby, lift, dining room for the Siblings and servants.
Floor 2: here are located the rooms of the service staff (humans), the servants. Cooks, cleaners, technical staff, employees of the recording and broadcasting rooms.
Floors 3-5: housing of the Siblings of Sin. 
Floors 6-8: Ritual Rooms, the Chapel, and rooms for entertainment and rest (a bar; a pool; a room with arcade machines; a room with a console and games; a gym.).
Floors 9 and 10: studios and rehearsal rooms.
Floor 11: The floor of V's personal Ghouls. Each of them lives in his own room, made to personal taste. But there is also a shared lounge.
Floors 12 and 13: The floors that belong to V. On the 12th floor is located Mateo's office, and V's "Sanctuary."

Grucifix Plaza:
A redesigned Babylon Court square. Every Wednesday, the Barter Market is held here, where you can trade something of your own for something more useful or just prettier. Pre-Purification relics, handmade furniture, paintings, cassettes and records sourced from somewhere, clothing and handmade jewelry... there's something for everyone!

The real Hollywood and Vine, a place where citizens assemble when they must. To discuss municipal issues, cleaning schedules, minor punishments for scoundrels — things that don't require Ghouls — discussing matters of upcoming holidays and festivals... V does not attend the meetings.

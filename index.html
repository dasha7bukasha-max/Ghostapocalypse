01 / The band

A spectacle
of sound.

Ghost is a Swedish rock band from Linköping, created by musician Tobias Forge. Since emerging in 2010, the band has built a world where heavy guitars meet soaring melodies and theatrical storytelling.

Onstage, a succession of larger-than-life frontman characters lead the masked Nameless Ghouls. Each era brings a new chapter to the story, but the invitation remains the same: come for the music, stay for the ritual.

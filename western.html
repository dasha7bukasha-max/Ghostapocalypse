IX

Western Domain

Ruled by Perpetua — all of the Americas.

Unholywood — a walled enclave (the former Hollywood). Now Papa V Perpetua rules it.

"V chose the city of false gods, where idols were made and worship was sold. Only here he is worse than the Devil."

There are no rules except those prescribed by the Tenets of Faith. It is an atmosphere of an eternal rock festival: celebrations are constantly taking place, music plays from everywhere, and Rituals are often held right in the streets, stripped of the ceremonial pomp inherent to Terzo's court. Siblings and servants and ordinary residents live here, eagerly baring their necks and thighs at the snap of V's fingers.

V's life can be divided into five periods: childhood, youth, adulthood, the Ghost era, and the Post-apocalypse. You can learn about Papa's past during your own roleplay (in the Nexus Trium bot and the Unholywood bot).

Venom Halo

Venom Halo was V's second project, his beloved brainchild, in which there was nothing sacred but everything sweet. V finally threw off his shackles and gained the freedom to create — the only thing his soul truly wanted.

Other Important Locations

Whisky a Go Go

It is a legendary small club on the Sunset Strip. It's V's frequent haunt, where he conducts meetings and rehearsals, ironically calling it his "throne room." The club is small, but it has a basement used for all matters requiring handcuffs or a cage.

The basement

Unholywood Bowl

The former Hollywood Bowl. Concerts are held here. People from all over the Domain are brought to the Monthly Mass — V wants everyone to have a chance to hear him live.

Walk of Fame

Hollywood streets that survived the Purification, the focus of life in Unholywood.

Sunset Strip

The legendary mile of clubs and bars (Whisky a Go Go among them).

Grucifix Plaza

A redesigned Babylon Court square. Every Wednesday the Barter Market is held here, where you can trade something of your own for something more useful or just prettier. Pre-Purification relics, handmade furniture, paintings, cassettes and records sourced from somewhere, clothing and handmade jewelry... there's something for everyone!

The Crossing

The real Hollywood and Vine, a place where citizens assemble when they must. To discuss municipal issues, cleaning schedules, minor punishments for scoundrels — things that don't require Ghouls — discussing matters of upcoming holidays and festivals... V does not attend the meetings.

The Triangle

The old corner tower at Sunset and Vine.

Hollywood Sign

Now a neon-purple Unholywood with two V's.
